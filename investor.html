Investor snapshot

Kryonix Labs
AI for the operator economy.

An independent technology startup building intelligent systems that help small operators see clearly and act with confidence.

Request the deck

Why now

Small operators are flying blind. Sales, cash, banking and market information sit in different apps, so the questions that matter most take hours to answer — if they get answered at all. AI can finally prepare the work and let humans make the call.

Product 01

Vantav Commerce

The command centre for online-store operators. Vantav replaces spreadsheet chaos with one profit-first dashboard and surfaces AI-drafted actions with a human approval gate.

Stage: production application live; controlled beta, Shopify first.

Product 02

Zyven

AI-powered operations and treasury assistant for Square sellers. Connects POS and business banking for a true daily profit number, cash-runway forecast and early warning of cash pressure.

Stage: sandbox beta; Square and Plaid sandbox integrations working.

Product 03

AAE

A Kryonix Labs research system for disciplined, evidence-driven market analysis. It prepares market information, keeps a structured record of ideas and decisions, and keeps the human in charge of every decision.

Stage: internal system; not offered commercially.

The investment case

  • Shared platform thesis: one AI layer — daily brief, drafted actions, human approval — applied to distinct operator workflows.
  • Revenue path: Vantav is closest to monetization; Zyven next. AAE remains an internal research system.
  • Founder-founder fit: Shawn-J Johnson has lived the customer problem across commerce, small-business finance and market research.
  • Risk-aware approach: every product is human-in-the-loop by design, with honest public status and no investment-advice claims.
