Why now

Organizations make more consequential decisions, across more teams and tools, than ever — and few can trace a decision from evidence to outcome.

Priority commercial product

Zyven

Cash-flow intelligence for businesses that take payments with Square, using read-only connections to Square and business bank accounts.

Stage: early access by application for a limited founding cohort.

Internal system

Aegis Alpha

Kryonix Labs' internal research and evidence system for market analysis. It keeps a structured record of hypotheses, decisions and outcomes, with a person in charge of every decision.

Stage: internal research system; not a live investment service and not offered commercially.

Commerce intelligence

Vantav

Commerce intelligence for online-store operators: sales, costs and true profit in one view.

Stage: paused while the company focuses on Zyven; not accepting new customers.

The investment case

  • Shared platform thesis: one decision-intelligence standard — evidence, coordination, human approval, verified outcomes — applied to distinct workflows. Kryonix Intelligence and Kyra run the company internally and are not sold.
  • Revenue path: Zyven is the priority commercial product. Vantav is paused. Aegis Alpha remains an internal research system.
  • Founder-market fit: Shawn-J Johnson has lived the customer problem across commerce, small-business finance and market research.
  • Risk-aware approach: every product is human-in-the-loop by design, with honest public status and no investment-advice claims.
