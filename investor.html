Investor snapshot

Kryonix Labs
AI for the operator economy.

An independent product studio building AI-powered tools that help small operators see clearly and act with confidence.

Request the deck

Why now

Small operators are flying blind. Sales, cash, banking and decisions sit in different apps, so the questions that matter most take hours to answer — if they get answered at all. AI can finally prepare the work and let humans make the call.

Product 01

Vantav Commerce

AI-powered storefronts for independent brands. Launch fast on top of an existing catalogue, then let Vantav surface the products, bundles and promotions most likely to convert.

Stage: live, partner approval stage.

Product 02

Zyvrn

AI-powered operations and treasury assistant for Square sellers. Connects POS and business banking for a true daily profit number, cash-runway forecast and one-tap money moves.

Stage: pre-MVP, waitlist open.

Product 03

AAE

Disciplined research and journaling workflow for serious individual traders. AAE scans a universe, prepares every number a trade needs and keeps an honest, automated journal.

Stage: internal tool, coming soon.

The investment case

  • Shared platform thesis: one AI layer — daily brief, drafted actions, human approval — applied to three distinct operator workflows.
  • Revenue path: Vantav is closest to monetization; Zyvrn next; AAE follows once the research engine proves repeatable value.
  • Founder-founder fit: Shawn-J Johnson has lived the customer problem across commerce, small-business finance and trading.
  • Risk-aware approach: every product is human-in-the-loop by design, with honest public status and no investment-advice claims.
