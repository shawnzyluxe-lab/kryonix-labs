Questions.

Do I need to know the solution first?

No. You do not need to know the solution before you begin. Describe what is not working and what you are trying to achieve.

Is the $3,500 a deposit?

No. The review is a complete deliverable for one bounded decision: evidence, options, a written recommendation and a readout.

What counts as one bounded decision?

One question your business has to answer, such as whether to automate invoicing or replace a scheduling tool. If it spans several linked decisions, we say so before you commit.

Does sending a request create an engagement or a charge?

No. We read every request and reply about whether a review fits. An engagement starts only with a signed agreement.

Is implementation included?

No. If you accept a recommendation that calls for implementation, it is scoped under a separate proposal and contract.

Do you give legal, regulatory or investment advice?

No. We evaluate evidence, exposure, vendors and controls. Jurisdiction-specific legal or regulatory conclusions need an appropriate specialist, and nothing we provide is investment advice.
