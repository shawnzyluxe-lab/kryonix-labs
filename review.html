Questions

Is there a charge to describe a challenge?

No. Before payment we only confirm the challenge, fit, who decides and the likely evidence; no findings, analysis or option comparisons.

How is the fee set?

It is fixed, and confirmed after qualification. You see the full scope, timeline and fee before you accept or pay.

When do the seven business days start?

After kickoff and receipt of the agreed evidence. If evidence arrives later, the date moves with it.

Is implementation included or credited?

No. The Review is a complete, standalone deliverable. Extended assessment and implementation are optional and separately contracted, and the fee is not automatically credited.

Legal, regulatory or investment advice?

No. Jurisdiction-specific legal or regulatory conclusions need a specialist, and nothing we provide is investment advice.
