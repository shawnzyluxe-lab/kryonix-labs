# Kryonix Labs — Investor One-Pager

## 1. The opportunity
Small operators are flying blind. Independent e-commerce brands, Square-based local businesses and individual traders all run on fragmented data. Sales, cash, banking and decisions sit in different apps, so the questions that matter most — "Did I make money today?", "How long is my runway?", "What should I trade next?" — take hours to answer, if they get answered at all.

Large companies solve this with analysts and dashboards. Small operators don't have that. They need AI assistants that prepare the work and let them make the call.

## 2. What we build
Kryonix Labs is an independent product studio building AI-powered tools for three high-friction operator workflows:

### Vantav Commerce — Commerce
AI-powered storefronts for independent brands. Launch fast on top of an existing catalogue, then let Vantav surface the products, bundles and promotions most likely to convert. Includes smart recommendations, a profit dashboard and margin alerts.

**Stage:** live, at partner approval stage.

### Zyvrn — Small-business finance
AI-powered operations and treasury assistant for Square sellers. Connects POS and business banking to give owners a true daily profit number, a cash-runway forecast and AI-drafted money moves they can approve in one tap.

**Stage:** pre-MVP, waitlist open.

### AAE — Trading research
A disciplined research and journaling workflow for serious individual traders. AAE scans a universe, prepares every number a trade needs and keeps an honest, automated journal of market, ideas and decisions. Human-in-the-loop by design.

**Stage:** internal tool, coming soon.

## 3. The model
Each product starts as a focused, paid SaaS. Common platform: AI-drafted insights, one-tap approvals, and a daily brief that turns scattered data into a short list of actions worth taking.

- **Vantav:** commerce tier + AI merchandising add-on.
- **Zyvrn:** subscription per connected location.
- **AAE:** freemium research tier + paid journal/automation plan.

All three share one design principle: **AI drafts, human decides.** Nothing runs on autopilot, which keeps risk low and trust high.
