# Kryonix Labs — Brand Kit

## Name
**Kryonix Labs** — always "Kryonix Labs" in prose (capital K, capital L). Shorthand: "Kryonix".
Never "KRYONIX" or "kryonix labs" in customer-facing copy (all-caps is reserved for the wordmark).

## What Kryonix means
**Kryonix** — precision technology engineered for complex systems.

## Tagline
**Intelligence, engineered.**

Supporting headline: **Where complex decisions become coordinated action.**

## Positioning statement
Kryonix Labs is an independent enterprise decision-intelligence technology company. We build intelligent systems that help organizations navigate complex operational decisions through evidence, coordination, human authority and verified outcomes.

## Voice
- Confident, calm, precise. A serious enterprise technology company, not an AI agency, startup portfolio or waitlist.
- Lead with evidence, coordination, human authority and verified outcomes.
- Our systems prepare and recommend; responsible people decide. Keep a human in the loop in the copy.
- State the vision plainly. Don't pad it with "we hope to" or "future company" disclaimers.
- Honest about status: only describe capabilities that are live, and label the rest "in development".
- Never claim enterprise clients, deployments, certifications, partnerships or launch dates without evidence.

### Words we use
decision intelligence · evidence · coordination · human authority · verified outcomes · commitments · approval · early access · internal system

### Words we avoid (customer-facing)
chatbot · automation agency · autonomous · COO / fractional COO · engine internals (orchestration, agents, pipelines) · signals / picks / guaranteed returns · any implied investment advice · capability claims for features not yet live (e.g. Zyven Money Moves)

## Logo
- `logo.svg` — hexagonal core with a stylised "K" and two node points. Use at 24px+.
- `wordmark.svg` — logo + "KRYONIX LABS". Use on dark backgrounds only; minimum width 180px.
- Clear space: at least the height of the hexagon on all sides.
- Do not recolour, rotate, add drop shadows, or place on busy imagery.
