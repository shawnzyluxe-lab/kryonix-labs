# Kryonix Labs Opportunity Priorities

**Horizon:** Next six weeks
**Rule:** Allocate founder time to the next piece of commercial evidence, not the product with the most features.

## Portfolio Decision

Run Kryonix as a two-product commercial portfolio with one protected research program:

1. **Vantav Commerce:** convert the deployed product into a focused paid merchant pilot.
2. **Zyven:** validate the cash-cliff workflow with paid design partners before broad construction.
3. **Aegis Alpha Research:** preserve the shadow trial and collect evidence; do not commercialize or expand scope before a `PROMOTE` verdict.
4. **Kryonix Labs:** provide governance, brand endorsement, entity readiness, access control, and portfolio reporting.

Do not start a fourth product.

## Priority Stack

| Rank | Opportunity | Why now | Next proof | Stop or redirect condition |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Vantav paid pilot | A deployed multi-channel product, live domain, pricing, beta flows, and operational modules already exist | One defined merchant segment, five completed onboardings, and three paying pilot merchants | No merchant completes setup or pays after focused outreach and onboarding repair |
| 2 | Zyven design-partner validation | The product has a clear Square-based cash-flow wedge but needs customer and forecast evidence | Five connected businesses, repeat use of the daily brief, measured forecast error, and three paid continuations | Owners do not recognize the cash-cliff problem or connected forecasts do not influence decisions |
| 3 | Portfolio access and identity | Missing repository access and undocumented brand relationships create execution and credibility risk across every venture | All repositories accessible to the operating account; founder-approved naming matrix; verified public claims | This is enabling work, so cap it rather than letting it replace customer work |
| 4 | Vantav/Zyven channel partnerships | Bookkeepers, fractional finance operators, and commerce consultants can distribute trusted operational tools | Three partner interviews and one partner-sourced design customer | Partners demand broad features before introducing customers |
| 5 | Aegis research evidence | The research program can become valuable only if its locked evaluation produces repeatable evidence | Complete the active shadow period and record the verdict without changing the trial | Any proposal requires changing an active trial, enabling live mode, or making public trading claims |

## Opportunity 1 — Vantav First Paid Cohort

### Current advantage

The local repository and deployed site show materially more than a concept:

- Multi-channel connectors and ingestion modules
- Profit and channel analytics
- Forecasting, alerts, and merchant approval flows
- Beta onboarding and test plans
- Published plan pricing
- A production domain

The immediate opportunity is commercialization, not another capability layer.

### Required focus

Choose one initial merchant profile. Recommended starting hypothesis:

> Shopify-led brands adding TikTok Shop and struggling to reconcile channel-level profit.

This is narrower and easier to prove than “all multi-channel merchants.” Validate it against the current users and partner requirements before adopting it publicly.

### Six-week objective

Convert three merchants to a paid, founder-led pilot using:

1. Guided channel connection
2. Verified true-profit baseline
3. Weekly margin-leak review
4. One merchant-approved corrective action
5. A documented before/after result

### Product direction

- Make channel-level true profit the entry workflow.
- Treat recommendations as the next layer, not the first promise.
- Measure onboarding time and missing-data rates.
- Make the Vantav Commerce and Prometheus OS relationship explicit after founder approval.
- Do not add more autonomous-agent breadth until merchants use existing alerts and approvals.

## Opportunity 2 — Zyven Cash-Cliff Validation

### Current advantage

Zyven can own a narrower daily question than general finance products:

> Is my operating cash safe through the next payroll and bill cycle?

The Square beachhead creates structured sales and payout data. The owner-confirmed obligation calendar creates information generic dashboards often miss.

### Six-week objective

Prove that five Square-powered cafes or quick-service operators:

1. Complete setup
2. Confirm obligations
3. Understand the forecast
4. Return to the daily brief
5. Change at least one decision
6. Pay to continue

### Product direction

- Keep financial math deterministic.
- Make data sources and assumptions visible.
- Track predicted versus actual balance.
- Keep scenarios simple and reversible.
- Defer autonomous actions and broad “ask anything” experiences.

## Opportunity 3 — Trusted Advisor Distribution

The strongest shared opportunity between Vantav and Zyven is distribution, not shared code.

Bookkeepers, ecommerce operators, fractional CFOs, and commerce consultants already have:

- Trusted customer relationships
- Access to fragmented operating data
- Recurring review cadences
- A need to monitor more businesses without adding manual spreadsheet work

Test whether an advisor can introduce and monitor three businesses through a lightweight partner view. Do not build a full partner portal before a partner produces customer introductions.

## Opportunities to Defer

### Shared Kryonix platform

Do not merge Vantav and Zyven backends, identity systems, data models, or agent frameworks. Shared infrastructure before product-market evidence adds coupling and increases security exposure. Share only operating standards, selected design tokens, vendor-negotiation leverage, and non-sensitive lessons.

### Autonomous financial operations

Do not move money, modify stores, or execute financial decisions without explicit customer approval. Autonomy raises trust, security, support, and partner-review requirements before the core insight is validated.

### Aegis commercialization

Do not add subscriptions, public API access, live brokerage execution, or broad marketing while the strategy is still in shadow evaluation.

### Fundraising campaign

Do not lead with a three-product fundraising narrative until:

- The legal entity and ownership are defined
- The customer-facing contracting entity is accurate across terms, privacy, security, checkout, and billing
- Naming risk is reviewed
- Vantav has current commercial evidence
- Zyven has customer validation
- Aegis status is represented accurately

Investor preparation can continue internally, but the public 60–90 day timeline should not remain unless the founder reconfirms it.

### Additional product ideas

Reject new ventures unless one of the current products is explicitly paused and the proposed opportunity has stronger customer evidence.

## Six-Week Operating Plan

### Week 1

- Confirm Vantav's actual launch blocker, connected channels, active users, and pricing readiness.
- Obtain the Zyven cross-account handoff and repository access.
- Approve an internal naming matrix; begin formal name review.
- Confirm whether `Vantav LLC` exists and is the correct customer-facing contracting entity.
- Recruit the first five interviews for each commercial venture.

### Week 2

- Select one Vantav merchant segment.
- Select one Zyven Square segment.
- Run founder-led onboarding for initial design customers.
- Establish baseline onboarding time and data-quality issues.

### Week 3

- Deliver Vantav's first weekly margin review.
- Deliver Zyven's first daily cash brief.
- Record decisions influenced and unresolved trust objections.

### Week 4

- Fix only repeated onboarding, data, accuracy, or decision-workflow failures.
- Start paid-pilot conversations.
- Interview three potential advisor partners.

### Week 5

- Collect payment or signed pilot commitments.
- Document one evidence-backed customer result per product if available.
- Decide whether advisor distribution merits a manual pilot.

### Week 6

- Apply the venture gates.
- Continue, narrow, pause, or redirect each commercial product.
- Update the Kryonix site only with verified names, statuses, and claims.
