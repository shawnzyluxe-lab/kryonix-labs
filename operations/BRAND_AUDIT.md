# Kryonix Labs Portfolio Brand Audit

**Status:** Internal recommendation. Do not change customer-facing names until the founder chooses the naming architecture and obtains appropriate legal review.

## Executive Assessment

Kryonix has a credible visual and verbal foundation: calm, precise, dark technical design; a human-approval principle; and the tagline **“Intelligence, engineered.”** The current weakness is portfolio clarity, not appearance.

Four issues should be resolved before investing further in the public brand:

1. The live finance product says **Zyven**, while the parent site says **Zyvrn**.
2. The parent site says **Vantav Commerce**, while the live domain presents **Prometheus OS**, without explaining whether one is the venture and the other is its platform.
3. The founder calls the trading venture **Aegis**, while the parent site says **AAE**.
4. Public materials present three products as one commercial portfolio even though Vantav, Zyven, and Aegis are at materially different validation and risk stages.

Separately, Vantav customer-facing policies and footers identify **Vantav LLC** as the operator. The founder has stated that the Kryonix Labs LLC is still planned. Confirm whether Vantav LLC legally exists before accepting contractual terms, subscriptions, or customer data under that name.

## Recommended Brand Architecture

Use an **endorsed portfolio**:

```text
Kryonix Labs
├── Vantav Commerce — commercial product
├── Zyven — commercial product in validation
└── Aegis Alpha — research program until promotion criteria are met
```

Each venture should keep its own product name and domain, with a consistent endorsement:

> A Kryonix Labs product

Kryonix should provide company trust, operating principles, and founder narrative. Product sites should sell one customer problem each. Do not force the same audience, visual accent, pricing, or go-to-market story across all three.

## Parent Brand

### Keep

- **Name:** Kryonix Labs
- **Tagline:** Intelligence, engineered.
- **Core voice:** confident, calm, precise, no hype
- **Operating principle:** AI prepares and explains; the customer decides
- **Visual system:** dark ink background with restrained cyan and violet accents

### Tighten

Current positioning spans commerce, small-business finance, and trading. The unifying idea should be decision quality, not a list of sectors:

> Kryonix Labs builds focused decision systems for independent operators.

Supporting line:

> We turn fragmented operating data into a clear picture, an early warning, and the next decision worth reviewing.

This accommodates Vantav and Zyven naturally. Aegis should be described as a research program until its shadow-mode evidence supports a commercial product claim.

### Avoid

- “AI-powered” as the main differentiator
- “Autonomous COO,” “autonomous CFO,” or similar role-replacement claims
- Treating three product cards as proof of three validated businesses
- Presenting Aegis research as a commercial trading product
- Fundraising timelines that have not been actively reconfirmed

## Product Names

### Vantav Commerce

**Recommendation:** Use **Vantav Commerce** as the venture and **Prometheus OS** as its product only if that distinction is intentional. State the relationship explicitly:

> Prometheus OS by Vantav Commerce

If the founder wants one customer-facing name, select it deliberately and retire the other from customer journeys. Do not leave visitors to infer the hierarchy.

The “Commerce” descriptor gives an otherwise abstract name immediate meaning. The product promise should remain profit and operational control for multi-channel merchants.

The live domain currently uses:

- `Prometheus OS` in the page title
- `Autonomous Commerce Intelligence` as a descriptor
- `The AI Operating System For Commerce` as the headline
- `Prometheus` or system terminology throughout the product surface

Without an endorsement, the parent-site promise and product experience look like separate companies. Keep internal engine and architecture codenames out of public copy even if `Prometheus OS` remains the approved product name.

Proposed one-line direction:

> Profit-first operations for multi-channel commerce.

The current copy exposes terms such as “engine,” “agents,” “matrix,” and “core.” Public copy should mask internal architecture and lead with accurate profit, unified channels, and merchant-approved actions. `Prometheus OS` can remain the product name without disclosing how the system is built.

Preliminary search found a `Vantav Technologies LLP` and commerce products using the shorter `Vant` name. This does not establish a conflict or availability. Formal entity, trademark, domain, app-store, and social-handle checks are still required.

### Zyven / Zyvrn

**Decision reported in the handoff:** The founder selected **Zyven** and purchased `thezyven.com`. Treat `Zyvrn` in the parent materials as stale pending a founder-approved public-copy change.

Zyven is operationally preferred because:

- It matches `thezyven.com`.
- It matches the live product.
- It is easier to pronounce and dictate than Zyvrn.
- The founder already uses it consistently.

However, preliminary search found active technology and finance-adjacent uses of “Zyven.” **Zyven is not cleared.** Do not make a major design, advertising, or incorporation investment until counsel performs a proper trademark and common-law review.

If Zyven cannot be protected, select a genuinely differentiated replacement rather than defaulting to Zyvrn solely because it is more unusual. Test pronunciation, spelling recall, domain availability, and trademark risk together.

Proposed category line:

> Cash-flow early warning for Square businesses.

### Aegis Alpha / AAE

**Recommendation:** Use **Aegis Alpha Research** internally and defer a final public commercial name.

“AAE” is opaque without explanation. “Aegis Alpha” communicates protection and trading performance, but preliminary search found:

- An existing `Aegis Alpha SA` technology company
- Aegis-branded trading and strategy-validation products
- Multiple finance and autonomous-trading uses of “Aegis”

The name is crowded and is not cleared. Because the product remains shadow-mode research, there is no need to force a public naming decision ahead of evidence.

Proposed current descriptor:

> A shadow-mode trading research and journaling program.

Do not use copy implying picks, signals, guaranteed alpha, managed money, autonomous execution, or investment advice.

## Portfolio Messaging Hierarchy

### Kryonix homepage

The homepage should answer, in order:

1. What does Kryonix build?
2. Who are the products for?
3. Which products are live, validating, or research-only?
4. Why should visitors trust the company?
5. Where should each visitor go next?

Recommended status labels:

| Venture | Public status |
|---|---|
| Vantav Commerce | Private beta or live, once current evidence is confirmed |
| Zyven | Design partner program or closed beta |
| Aegis Alpha Research | Internal research; not publicly available |

Avoid using “live,” “production,” “waitlist,” “early access,” or partner-status claims unless the portfolio register records an owner, date, and evidence link for the status.

### Product sites

Each site should use:

1. One audience
2. One urgent problem
3. One primary outcome
4. One status-appropriate call to action
5. One evidence section
6. One Kryonix endorsement

Recommended calls to action:

- Vantav: `Apply for the merchant pilot`
- Zyven: `Become a design partner`
- Aegis: no commercial call to action until promotion

## Claim Audit

### Claims needing verification

- Zyven quantified outcome statements
- Zyven customer testimonial
- Vantav “live operations platform in production”
- Vantav partner-approval status
- Vantav pricing and plan readiness
- Prometheus/Vantav product identity
- Vantav LLC as the legal operator in terms, privacy, security, refund, checkout, and footer copy
- Zyven waitlist status
- AAE early-access availability
- Investor-material claims about the technical foundation
- A 60–90 day fundraising timeline

Unverified claims should be removed, dated and sourced internally, or explicitly labeled as illustrative sandbox data.

### Safe claim pattern

Prefer:

> Zyven projects upcoming cash pressure using connected and owner-confirmed financial data.

Avoid:

> Zyven prevents cash shortfalls.

The first describes product behavior. The second implies an outcome that requires customer evidence.

## Preliminary Name-Risk Scan

This is a web-search screen, not legal clearance.

| Name | Preliminary finding | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Kryonix Labs | `Kryonix` is used by a US cooling-infrastructure company, a Singapore automation company, and a UK software company | Run US federal/state and common-law review before LLC and major promotion |
| Vantav Commerce | `Vantav Technologies LLP` exists; shorter `Vant` commerce branding is active | Review software, commerce, and SaaS classes |
| Zyven | Multiple technology uses and a finance-adjacent Zyven brand appear in search | Treat as urgent; decide before public launch |
| Zyvrn | Fewer obvious exact search results | Still run legal, phonetic, and confusing-similarity review |
| Aegis Alpha | Exact technology-company use and crowded Aegis trading usage | Do not commercialize under the name without review |
| AAE | Highly ambiguous abbreviation | Avoid as the primary customer-facing name |

At minimum, professional clearance should cover:

- USPTO records and pending applications
- State business registries
- Common-law web usage
- Relevant international markets
- Domains and app stores
- Social handles
- Similar spelling, sound, and meaning
- The product and service classes Kryonix expects to enter

## Recommended Brand Decisions

1. Confirm the parent architecture: endorsed products under Kryonix Labs.
2. Complete legal review of the selected `Zyven` brand and replace stale `Zyvrn` copy only after founder approval.
3. Treat Aegis as a Kryonix research program until a strategy earns promotion.
4. Confirm Vantav's real current commercial status and define the relationship between Vantav Commerce and Prometheus OS.
5. Remove or update the 60–90 day fundraising language unless it is still an active, evidence-backed objective.
6. Require a source and review date for every status, testimonial, and quantified product claim.
7. Verify the contracting entity and update every legal surface with qualified legal review before accepting customers or payments.

## Implementation Order

After founder approval:

1. Update the brand glossary and product status table.
2. Update page titles, metadata, navigation, form options, product cards, and footers.
3. Align the product sites and parent site.
4. Add consistent “A Kryonix Labs product” endorsements.
5. Replace unsupported claims with validated evidence or explicit sandbox labels.
6. Update investor materials only after the company, fundraising plan, product stages, and customer evidence are current.
