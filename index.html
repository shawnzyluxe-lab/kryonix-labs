Cash-flow intelligence · Commercial product

Zyven

Early access by application

Cash-flow intelligence for businesses that take payments with Square. Zyven connects Square sales and business bank accounts, then shows each morning what the business kept, how many days of cash it has, and the one move worth considering, with the numbers and assumptions behind every call.

  • Daily view of what the business actually kept
  • A 90-day cash picture that shows when money gets tight
  • Read-only connections; nothing moves without the owner's approval

We're admitting a limited number of founding businesses. Applying is free; accepted businesses receive an invitation to sign up.

Internal systems

How Kryonix Labs itself runs. These systems are integral to the company and are not offered as commercial products.

Internal system

Kryonix Intelligence

The company's decision-intelligence core. It keeps one record of objectives, evidence, commitments, approvals and outcomes, so decisions can be traced to the evidence behind them and checked against what actually happened.

Internal system

Kyra

The Founder's executive assistant, working on top of Kryonix Intelligence. Kyra organises priorities, prepares decisions and follows up on commitments; approvals stay with the Founder.

How we build

01

Evidence first

Every recommendation shows the data and assumptions behind it. If the evidence is missing, we say so instead of guessing.

02

Coordinated work

Decisions connect to the commitments, owners and follow-ups they create, so nothing important depends on memory.

03

Human authority

Our systems prepare and recommend. The person responsible approves, and nothing consequential happens without that approval.

04

Verified outcomes

We check whether a decision produced the result it promised, and we report it honestly when it didn't.

About Kryonix Labs

Kryonix Labs is an independent decision-intelligence technology company. We build systems that help people make consequential decisions with clear evidence, coordinated follow-through and a record of what actually happened.

We run the company on our own internal systems first, and we're building for long-term growth: starting with Zyven for small businesses, and designed so the same standard can serve larger organisations over time.
